# Business Insights

## 1. Overall Hospital Performance

The hospital emergency dataset contains 25 total visits from 20 unique patients.

A total of 10 doctors handled these visits.

The overall average waiting time was 24.64 minutes, while the average treatment time was 167.16 minutes (approximately 2 hours 47 minutes).

## 2. Doctor Workload

Doctor-wise analysis shows that Dr. Amit Sharma handled the highest number of visits with 4 visits.

Dr. Rahul Deshmukh, Dr. Vikram Kulkarni, Dr. Priya Patil, and Dr. Arjun More each handled 3 visits.

The remaining doctors handled between 1 and 2 visits.

This analysis helps understand how patient workload is distributed among doctors.

## 3. Department Performance

The Emergency department recorded the highest number of patient visits with 7 visits.

General Medicine, Cardiology, Neurology, and Orthopedics each recorded 3 visits.

Pulmonology and Pediatrics recorded 2 visits each, while Gynecology and Surgery recorded 1 visit each.

## 4. Patient Waiting Time

The overall average waiting time was 24.64 minutes.

Doctor-wise analysis shows that Dr. Vikram Kulkarni had the highest average waiting time of 45 minutes.

Dr. Sneha Singh had an average waiting time of 37.50 minutes, while Dr. Pooja Shah had 35 minutes.

These results help identify differences in waiting time across doctors.

## 5. Triage Analysis

The average waiting time varied across triage levels.

- Low: 45.00 minutes
- Medium: 30.56 minutes
- High: 16.50 minutes
- Critical: 7.25 minutes

The Critical triage group had the lowest average waiting time in this dataset, while the Low triage group had the highest.

## 6. Treatment Time

The overall average treatment time was 167.16 minutes.

Cardiology had the highest average treatment time at 263 minutes.

Neurology had an average treatment time of 211 minutes, while Emergency had 193.86 minutes.

General Medicine had the lowest average treatment time among the departments at 80 minutes.

## 7. Long Treatment Cases

The longest treatment duration recorded was 348 minutes for Abhishek Yadav in the Neurology department.

Other long treatment cases included:

- Vivek Kulkarni — 343 minutes — Cardiology
- Pooja Verma — 288 minutes — Emergency
- Pooja Verma — 282 minutes — Emergency
- Vivek Kulkarni — 278 minutes — Cardiology

These cases can be further investigated to understand reasons for longer treatment durations.

## 8. Visit Status

Out of 25 total visits:

- 17 visits (68%) were Discharged.
- 8 visits (32%) were Admitted.

This provides an overview of the outcomes recorded in the emergency visit dataset.

## 9. Diagnosis Analysis

Diagnosis-wise analysis shows that several diagnoses occurred more frequently than others.

Fracture, Migraine, Trauma, and Infection each had 2 unique patients in the visible analysis results.

Other diagnoses had 1 unique patient each.

## 10. Department-wise Doctor Workload

The workload ranking shows:

- Emergency: Dr. Amit Sharma ranked 1st with 4 visits.
- Emergency: Dr. Arjun More ranked 2nd with 3 visits.
- Cardiology: Dr. Priya Patil ranked 1st with 3 visits.
- General Medicine: Dr. Vikram Kulkarni ranked 1st with 3 visits.
- Orthopedics: Dr. Rahul Deshmukh ranked 1st with 3 visits.

The remaining departments had one doctor in the available dataset.

## 11. Daily Visit Trends

Daily visit trends were analyzed using patient arrival dates.

The daily visit count can be used to identify dates with higher or lower patient activity.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that the dataset contains 25 emergency visits involving 20 patients and 10 doctors.

The Emergency department recorded the highest number of visits, while Dr. Amit Sharma handled the highest number of visits.

The overall average waiting time was 24.64 minutes and the average treatment time was 167.16 minutes.

The analysis also identified differences in waiting time, treatment duration, department workload, triage levels, diagnoses, and visit outcomes.
